Exact URLs, source-path line ranges, classifications and limits are attached to each linked [claim](CLAIMS_LEDGER.md). ## Calendar and counting discrepancies

- The actual Events body says no active events, while the developer's official experience description advertises Halloween and the owner reports Halloween. Treat the wiki index as incomplete for current availability. Neither the public page nor retained Halloween26 definitions proves this account's prerequisites or an exact event end date. [W-C032](CLAIMS_LEDGER.md#w-c032), [R-C001](CLAIMS_LEDGER.md#r-c001).
- Autumn 2025 and Autumn 2026 have separate removed banners, dates and currency schemas. The 2026 main-period and Harvest subsection dates may refer to separate phases. Bubble Pass, Challenge Pass, guild season and event calendars must remain separate. [W-C033](CLAIMS_LEDGER.md#w-c033), [W-C034](CLAIMS_LEDGER.md#w-c034), [M-C049](CLAIMS_LEDGER.md#m-c049), [M-C051](CLAIMS_LEDGER.md#m-c051).
- Guilds says no active season/Season 1 ended October 10. Saved Season 2 declares October 9–November 8 and WeekDuration is six days. Neither catalog nor wiki establishes live activation. [R-C013](CLAIMS_LEDGER.md#r-c013).
- Board Game 87 tiles versus 88 physical native nodes may be a counting/version difference. Wiki pet/currency totals also use uncertain inclusion scopes. These are not proof of missing game entries. [W-C023](CLAIMS_LEDGER.md#w-c023), [E-C019](CLAIMS_LEDGER.md#e-c019), [R-C016](CLAIMS_LEDGER.md#r-c016).
- Removed 2025 **Shadow Realm** is separate from permanent 2026 **The Shadow Realm**. Native world-registry absence does not disprove a secondary hub, but its current entry/purchase contract remains unverified. [W-C014](CLAIMS_LEDGER.md#w-c014).
- Launch guides' three Coins-only Mastery branches and numbered Overworld “worlds” are historical. They do not describe the current eight-branch, multi-currency game. [R-C002](CLAIMS_LEDGER.md#r-c002), [R-C003](CLAIMS_LEDGER.md#r-c003).
