## Phase 4–9 integration and release boundaries

| Phase | Research inputs / future contribution | Required live acceptance and stop conditions |
|---|---|---|
| 4 — Pets/hatching/inventory | Owned optimum, native pet projection, conditional pools, variants, protection/crafting | Explicit Phase4 approval precedes implementation. Verify equips, hatch debit/count/cadence, inventory outcome and protected IDs. Preserve owner-tested bubbling/selling/stationary background location ownership. Stop on stale inventory/full storage/unknown paid policy or odds needed for spending. |
| 5 — Mastery/rewards | All branch tiers, complete prerequisites, rate/horizon payback, ready claims | Verify purchased level and debit; independent actual rate gain. Verify daily/playtime claims, milestone completion/effective buffs, known Index claimed fields and credited items; ordinary milestone grant/claim timing remains unestablished. Never substitute animation or request return. Stop on unknown identifier/cooldown/reserve. |
| 6 — Quests/shops/consumables | Objective overlap, restock/stock pricing, use-count limits, protected allocation | Verify accepted quest/counter/claim, exact purchased stock and consumption; protect reserve and rare potion/craft resources. Stop on changed slot or unknown batch semantics. |
| 7 — Advanced worlds/rifts/minigames | Native gate-origin graph, expiring rifts, renewal models, fish/FishDex, legitimate obby state | Owner validates each new travel/unlock/approach and activity action. Required geometry/course/shore observations remain gaps. Stop on expiry, unsupported movement/state, missing reward result or repeated failures. No pickup chasing or fabricated routes. |
| 8 — Seasonal | Separate year-specific keys, current-season evidence, end times, protected rebirth/shops | Owner confirms current account/event eligibility and any reset economics; actual expiry and debit/rebirth result. Stop when identity/availability inconsistent or event expires. Fall2025/Fall2026/Halloween2026 stay distinct. |
| 9 — Full ROI Brain | Goal-time comparison, uncertainty, rates, reserve vectors, candidate arbitration/explainability | One central authority with lifecycle/verification. Hybrid farming remains deferred to this phase and needs its own owner approval/live scope. Preserve Phase1–3 behavior; abstain when irreversible/protected effects unknown. |

Generic roadmap build/commit/push instructions do not apply to this research-only assignment. GitHub remains suspended; these proposals were saved locally, not published. No auto-trade, Robux purchase, new action loop or gameplay test was introduced.
