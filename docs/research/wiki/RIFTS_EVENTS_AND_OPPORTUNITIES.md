## Productive Rift lifetime and net resources

An egg Rift is useful when it improves feasible owned-team outcomes or a defined hatch/index/bounty objective. Its displayed luck and rarity alone are insufficient.

Let H be actual object expiry, t synchronized native time, d total approach/setup/verification allowance and m a margin. Productive budget is max(0,H-t-d-m). With supported affordable batch b and measured accepted cycle time T, a conditional upper bound is b*floor(productiveBudget/T), further bounded by currency, space and item commitments. Partial-cycle setup or delayed results can reduce realized hatches. The server's actual NextEggOpenAt/result pacing overrides an assumed cycle.
